# Roadmap / 路线图

## English

The roadmap is directional, not a release commitment.

- P0: product presentation, explicit standard/test/demo modes, safe initialization and diagnostics, synthetic demo reset, documentation, community files, Windows CI, and public-release policy self-tests.
- P1 candidates: accessibility and keyboard-flow audits, richer import/export of non-sensitive structured records, and clearer diagnostics for supported mail providers.
- P2 candidates: additional local analytics and optional integrations that preserve loopback operation, least privilege, and human review.

Permanent constraints: no final-submit automation, no unattended bulk applying, no raw-mail inbox, no plaintext credential fallback, and no public candidate data.

## 中文

路线图只表示方向，不构成版本承诺。

- P0：完善产品呈现，明确正式/测试/Demo 模式，提供安全初始化与环境自检、合成 Demo 重置、文档、社区文件、Windows CI 和公开发布策略自测。
- P1 候选：无障碍与键盘流程审查、非敏感结构化记录的导入导出，以及更清晰的受支持邮箱诊断。
- P2 候选：更多本地分析，以及仍能保持 loopback、最小权限和人工复核的可选集成。

长期约束：不自动最终提交、不无人值守海投、不提供原始邮件收件箱、不回退到明文凭据、不公开候选人数据。
